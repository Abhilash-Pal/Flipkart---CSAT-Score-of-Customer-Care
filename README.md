# Flipkart---CSAT-Score-of-Customer-Care
Mean CSAT Score is 5.54 with 11,214 users (37%) who gave a score. Feedback participation is low.
More customers have negative sentiment -> 15,526 out of which 5,825 users scored with mean CSAT 3.79. Users with positive sentiments are 6,471 out of which 2394 scored with mean CSAT of 8.65. Increase participation from customers with more positive sentiment by sending reminders over mail & app notifications.
Kolkata call centre performs significantly poorly on CSAT Score with mean of 5.46. Give adequate training & other facilitates at Kolkata call centre. Chennai performs well with mean score of 5.63 but attends lowest no. of users. Try to accomodate more users at Chennai call centre. 
Chatbot & Email affects the mean CSAT score. Either improve the the service or urge users to use the web & call-centre channels more.
Wrt to response time, cases below SLA surprisingly has a higher mean CSAT compared to within SLA.
Calls lasting 11-20 minutes achieved the highest mean CSAT score of 5.61.
Thursday has significantly higher issues with lowest CSAT score of 5.43. Wednesday has the highest score of 5.61. Highest complaints come during Tuesdays through Fridays so allocate more resources & personnels on those days.
Billing related questions has significantly the highest number of issues. They are an important parameter as it could potentially improve the overall experience & thereby the mean CSAT score.
Karnataka, MP, Haryana & Punjab seems to have a CSAT score below the mean.
